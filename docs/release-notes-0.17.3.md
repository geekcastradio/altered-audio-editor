## Altered Audio Editor 0.17.3

A small fix release.

### Fixes
- **Greyed-out menu items now tell you why.** Clicking a menu item that is unavailable used to do nothing at all. It now shows the reason at the bottom of the window, such as "Select one music clip" for Clip > Fit Music to Length.
- **Fit Music to Length** no longer lets you try to fit a clip to the length it already has. The box opens on the clip's own length, so Find Joins is held back until you type a new one, with a note saying why.
- **Stretch in the Properties panel** now uses the clearer names. The section is called Stretch, and its Mode choices read Off, Real-time and Rendered (HQ). Saved sessions are not affected.
