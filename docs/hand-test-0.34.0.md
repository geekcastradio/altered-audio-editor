# Hand test 0.34.0: Play Selected Area, Soundboard, Automation page

## Play Selected Area
1. Open a waveform, show the Spectrum view and draw a selection around one band of sound.
2. Choose Effects > Spectrum > Play Selected Area. You hear only what is inside the selection. The file is not changed and Undo is unchanged.
3. With no selection the item is unavailable.
4. Try a selection that spans several minutes: a notice asks for a shorter stretch (up to five minutes).

## Soundboard
1. Open a session. View > Panels > Soundboard.
2. Press an empty pad, choose a short sound. The pad takes the file's name.
3. Select a track, put the playhead somewhere, press the pad. The sound lands at the playhead on that track. Undo removes it in one step.
4. With no track selected, press the pad: it goes on the first track.
5. Change and Clear work. Assign a key to Multitrack > Soundboard > Pad 1 in Keyboard Shortcuts and press it.
6. Close and reopen the program: the pads are still there.
7. Move a pad's file, press the pad: a notice says none of the files could be read and nothing breaks.
8. In a waveform file the pad actions are unavailable.

## Settings > Automation
1. Open Settings > Automation. Two notes show. No controls.
