# Altered Audio Editor 0.35.0

Playback speed, for listening and reviewing. Experimental and off until you turn it on.

## What's new

- **Playback speed.** Settings > Playback and Recording > Playback speed adds Transport > Playback Speed, from 0.5x to 2x with the pitch kept. It works in the multitrack and the Waveform editor, with loops and skips, and the playhead and meters keep in step.

## Good to know

- It is for listening only. Recording, exports and saved files are not affected.
- 0.5x and 2x sound smeared. 0.75x to 1.5x suits speech.
- The metronome is silent while the speed is not Normal.
- It does not apply to 3D Space sessions heard on speakers.
- Faders in a session with buses or sends are heard after about half a second at any speed other than Normal.
- It starts at Normal each time the program starts, and recording puts it back to Normal.
- Normal playback is unchanged and uses the same path as 0.34.
