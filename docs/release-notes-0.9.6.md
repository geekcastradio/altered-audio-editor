# Altered Audio Editor 0.9.6

Polish, and a way in from Audition.

- **Rounded corners** on menus, dialogs and fields, a slight rounding like Audition's.
- **Readable drop-downs.** Preset lists no longer show black text on dark.
- **Effect windows** put their how-to text behind an (i) in the title bar, and the On/Off text is now the same power button as the Voice Chain rows.
- **Amp Model** now matches the rest of the app.
- **Import Chains from Audition** (Voice Chain > Presets): bring in the effect chains from Audition's `EffectPresets.xml`. You get the same effects in the same order with our starting values; limiter ceilings carry over. Effects without an equivalent are listed and skipped.
- **Favorites.** Star an effect in its window to find it at the top of every effect menu.
