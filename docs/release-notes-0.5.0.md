# Altered Audio Editor 0.5.0

Ready-made starting points for Delay and Echo.

## New
- **Delay presets** in the Voice Chain's preset list, under "Delay (add one effect)": Slapback Echo, Podcast Room Echo, Ping-Pong Echo and Long Dark Echo. Each adds one Delay and Echo effect and leaves the rest of the chain alone. Change any setting afterward and the preset name clears, like the other presets.

## Test checklist
- Open the Voice Chain, choose each Delay preset, and confirm one Delay and Echo effect is added with the settings you expect
- Play speech through each: Slapback is a tight doubling, Room Echo is subtle, Ping-Pong bounces left and right, Long Dark is long and muffled
- Choose a Delay preset on a chain that already has effects and confirm they stay
