## 0.31.0

### Fixed

- Opening a video from the Files panel, or dragging one onto it, now shows its picture in the Video panel. Before, only the panel's own Open button did.
- The Video panel says what went wrong when it cannot read a picture, instead of a generic message.
- Stop in the Waveform editor leaves the cursor where playback stopped. The Multitrack still returns to where playback started.

### New

- The Multitrack shows a Video Reference bar under the ruler. It follows the zoom and can be dragged to change when the picture starts.
- Session tabs: with two or more sessions open, a tab for each runs along the top of the Multitrack.
- Windows: an MSI installer is now published next to the setup program, for schools and managed machines.
