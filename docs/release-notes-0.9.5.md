# Altered Audio Editor 0.9.5

Settings you can keep, a calmer Libraries panel, and a guided sound tab.

## Your settings are safe
- **Backup and Restore** (Settings): save everything to one `.aaebackup` file, restore it in Merge (keep mine, add what is missing) or Replace mode, and pick which kinds to restore. Passwords and tokens are left out unless you choose otherwise.
- **Automatic snapshots** at launch and before each upgrade, kept in Documents (or beside the app in portable mode), newest ten by default, with an optional second copy in a sync folder.
- **Portable mode**: put a file named `portable` beside the app and it keeps all settings in a `data` folder next to it.
- **Import shortcuts from Audition**: Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts > Import from Audition. Reports what it could not map.

## Look
- Panels are lighter by default. Settings > Appearance > Panel brightness has Classic to go back.

## Libraries
- Sections instead of chips: group by bin or type, sort by name or date, filter, list or tiles, and the view is remembered.
- Pictures (JPEG, PNG) live in the library with thumbnails. Drag one onto the Metadata panel's cover slot to set MP3 cover art.

## Audio FX and Sound Types
- Audio FX applies as you click, as one undo step, with tooltips instead of paragraphs.
- New Sound Types tab: Dialogue, Music, SFX, Ambience, with jobs to tick, a preset menu and one Amount slider. It shows Custom if you change the effects in the Voice Chain.

## Layout
- Workspace switcher beside the menu bar.
- Status bar shows sample rate, bit depth and free disk space.
- Alt+1 to Alt+0 show or hide panels.
- Dynamics has a graph and six built-in presets.
