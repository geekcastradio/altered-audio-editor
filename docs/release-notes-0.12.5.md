# Altered Audio Editor 0.12.5

Built-in presets for four effects. Nothing else changed.

- **Limiter (10 presets).** Stop at -0.1 dB, three Lift-and-stop ceilings (-0.1, -3 and -6 dB), four Squeeze strengths from Light to Crushed, Wall of Loud, and a -1 dB Streaming Ceiling. Input boost tops out at 24 dB in this limiter, so the loudest presets sit at that limit.
- **Speech Leveler (5 presets).** Even Out in five strengths, from Full to Tight, with the compressor and gate set to match.
- **Parametric EQ (13 presets).** Voice cleanup, voice presence and mud cut, rumble and hiss cuts, a telephone band, a vintage broadcast curve, a loudness lift, guitar, kick and snare shapes, and a Flat reset. Each preset sets every control, so applying one fully replaces the last.
- **Dynamics (11 more, 17 in all).** Sibilance control in three strengths, vocal and narration compressors, two ceiling limiters, a dense bass setting and a gentle mix bus.
- Built-in presets are listed apart from your own and cannot be edited or deleted. Save your own from the same menu as before.
- Tests now check every built-in preset against the effect's real ranges, so a preset can't ship with a value the knob can't reach.
