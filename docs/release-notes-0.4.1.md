# Altered Audio Editor 0.4.1

The first delay effect, and a tidier way to clear plug-in windows off the screen.

## New
- **Delay and Echo** (Effects > Delay and Reverb). Delay time (1 ms to 2 s), Feedback, Tone (darkens the repeats as they fade), Echo level and Ping-pong (repeats bounce between left and right). The dry sound always passes at full level. No added latency. Changing the delay time while playing glides to the new time and bends the repeats like tape instead of clicking.
- **Hide All Effect Windows** (Effects menu). Closes the effect settings window and any open plug-in windows in one go.

## Test checklist
- Add Delay and Echo from Effects > Delay and Reverb; clap or speak and hear repeats at the set time
- Raise Feedback: repeats last longer but always die out; lower Tone: repeats get darker
- Turn on Ping-pong on a stereo track: repeats alternate sides
- Drag Delay time during playback: no clicks, repeats bend smoothly
- Open the effect window and a plug-in window, choose Hide All Effect Windows: both close
