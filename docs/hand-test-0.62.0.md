# Hand test for 0.62.0

1. Spectrum view: open a file, draw a selection with Box, Lasso or Brush. A Play button shows on the bar over it. Click it and only the selected area plays.
2. Right-click in the Spectrum view. Play Selected Area is the first item. It is greyed out with no selection and plays the area with one.
3. Help menu: Ask a Question or Share an Idea sits under the GitHub item and opens the Discussions page in your browser.
4. Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts: record a few chords, including ones with Ctrl, Shift, Alt and F keys. They save and show as before.
5. Open a session with a tempo change and a MIDI track: the grid, bar numbers and playback timing are unchanged.
6. Open a multichannel file (stereo is enough). Clip > Source Channels: the quick settings list shows and picking one plays as labelled.
7. Waveform editor: switch a channel off, apply an effect, then paste. Only the channels still on change. Solo a channel, then solo it again to bring all back.
8. Zoom and snap clips to each other and to markers; stretch a clip with Alt+drag. Lengths and edges look as before.
9. macOS (Apple silicon) testers: the DMG is in the download list.
10. Help > Check for Updates still works.
