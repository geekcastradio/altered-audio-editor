- Offer the official release builds only, as published on this project's GitHub releases page, unmodified.
- Keep the app as we ship it: its own updater, its own settings and its own folders. Do not replace the updater, change where it stores settings, or add tracking, accounts, ads or bundled extras to it.

This permission is for listing the unmodified official app. It can be withdrawn for anyone who breaks these terms. Anything beyond it needs written permission from The GeekCast Radio Network, LLC.
